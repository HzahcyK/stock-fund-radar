# Scoring Rubric

Use scoring to make market calls auditable. Scores are decision aids, not mechanical trading signals.

## Market Regime Score

Score 0-100:

- Global politics, major events, and risk appetite, 15: overseas equities/futures, volatility, geopolitical stress, wars/conflicts, sanctions/export controls, trade/diplomatic events, major summits/meetings, and whether the news is confirmed or already priced in.
- Global liquidity, 15: US yields, dollar, central-bank tone, commodity/rate shocks.
- China policy and liquidity, 15: fiscal/monetary/regulatory support, PBOC operations, policy credibility, and implementation path.
- A-share tape and technical structure, 15: volume, breadth, index structure, style leadership, limit-up quality, major-index K-lines, moving-average position, support/resistance, and whether hot-sector K-lines confirm the narrative.
- Capital flow, 20: sector/concept main funds, ETF flows, north/southbound data where available, margin clues.
- Fundamentals, earnings, and valuation, 20: latest reports, earnings revisions, product price cycle, margins, cash flow, inventory, order visibility, valuation, and crowding.

Interpretation:

- 0-30: defensive. Favor cash-like, short-duration bond, dividend/low-volatility, or wait.
- 31-50: weak balance. Use small tactical exposure only with confirmation.
- 51-70: constructive. Look for mainline themes with confirmed flows.
- 71-100: aggressive but watch crowding. Avoid chasing one-day extremes.

## Theme Scoring

Score each sector/theme 0-5 on:

- Policy support: direct, recent, and funded policy beats vague slogans.
- Event catalyst quality: geopolitical/policy/summit/news catalyst is current, verified, investable, and has a clear sector transmission path.
- Earnings logic: visible revenue/profit improvement, order cycle, price/product cycle, margin expansion, cash-flow improvement, or cost decline.
- Financial report confirmation: latest reports or guidance from representative companies support the sector thesis.
- Valuation/earnings match: recent price move is not wildly ahead of earnings visibility, or the risk is explicitly flagged.
- Macro fit: rate, FX, commodity, consumption, capex, or geopolitics alignment.
- Capital confirmation: multi-day main-fund/ETF inflow and broad participation.
- Technical structure: relative strength, breakout from base, volume expansion, low overhead supply.
- K-line confirmation: daily/weekly trend, MA5/10/20/60 alignment, breakout or pullback quality, support/resistance, gap behavior, long upper/lower shadows, and whether price is extended.
- Momentum indicators: MACD direction/cross/divergence, KDJ overbought/oversold or turning points, RSI strength or divergence. Use indicators as confirmation, not as standalone signals.
- Crowding risk: lower score if already parabolic, narrative saturated, or valuation stretched.
- Fund implementability: good ETFs/index funds/active funds exist with adequate liquidity and clean exposure.

Quiet-start candidates usually score high in policy/earnings/technical but are not yet top-ranked by media heat. Require at least one flow confirmation before presenting as tactical.

## Evidence-Chain Rating

Rate each重点方向 before recommending:

- Policy/macro: 0-5
- Geopolitical/event catalyst: 0-5
- Industry cycle: 0-5
- Financial report/earnings proof: 0-5
- Valuation/crowding: 0-5
- Capital flow: 0-5
- Technical structure: 0-5
- Fund implementability: 0-5

Classify:

- 32-40: mainline candidate. Can be held or added on technical triggers.
- 24-31: tactical candidate. Use small position or wait for confirmation.
- 16-23: watchlist only. Needs more evidence.
- Below 14: avoid unless the user explicitly wants a speculative trade.

## Event and Technical Gating

- No strong "mainline" call if the event/policy catalyst is stale, unverified, or contradicted by the tape.
- No "add" call for a hot sector unless its K-line setup is stated: above/below MA5/10/20/60, volume quality, support/resistance, and momentum or divergence.
- If capital flow is strong but K-line is parabolic, downgrade to "hold/watch pullback" unless there is a fresh catalyst that justifies acceleration.
- If policy/geopolitics is bullish but funds are flowing out and price is below MA20/60, classify as "logic exists, timing not confirmed".

## Technical Analysis Checklist

For each重点板块 or recommended ETF, check:

- Trend: is the sector/index above MA5/10/20/60, and are short averages turning upward?
- Pattern: base breakout, first pullback after breakout, range compression, trend continuation, or failed breakout.
- Volume: breakout with volume expansion is stronger; rising price with shrinking volume needs caution; high-volume long upper shadow often means near-term supply.
- Relative strength: compare with Shanghai Composite, CSI 300, ChiNext, STAR 50, and the closest peer sector.
- Support/resistance: identify the nearest support, pressure level, previous high/low, gap, and invalidation line.
- Momentum: use MACD/KDJ/RSI to spot acceleration, exhaustion, or divergence.
- ETF confirmation: check ETF premium/discount if available, turnover, intraday trend, and whether ETF volume supports the sector signal.

Interpretation:

- Strong setup: policy/industry catalyst plus multi-day flow, price above key averages, volume-supported breakout, and no obvious momentum divergence.
- Watchlist setup: good catalyst and improving flow, but price is still below MA20/60 or facing major resistance.
- Avoid chasing: one-day spike, stretched above MA5/10, high-volume upper shadow, or momentum divergence after a large move.

## Fund Ranking

Rank funds by intended role:

- Core allocation: broad index, low fee, high liquidity/AUM, diversified exposure, low tracking error.
- Satellite theme: clear thematic purity, reasonable liquidity, acceptable concentration, catalyst alignment.
- Active alpha: manager consistency, drawdown control, repeatable process, style fit, and holdings matching the thesis.
- Overseas/QDII: underlying market setup, premium/discount, FX risk, quota/subscription status, and settlement delay.
- Defensive/hedge: money market, short bond, gold/commodity, low-volatility dividend, or currency exposure.

Use a 5-point score for each fund:

- Thesis fit
- Liquidity/AUM
- Cost/tracking/manager quality
- Risk control/drawdown
- Timing, technical structure, and subscription availability

Prefer "watchlist with trigger" over "buy now" when timing confidence is weak.
