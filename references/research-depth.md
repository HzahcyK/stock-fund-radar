# Research Depth

Use this reference when the user asks for deeper judgment, mainline prediction, hidden opportunities, portfolio action, or says the analysis is shallow/formulaic.

## Evidence Chain

For each重点方向, build a compact but real evidence chain:

1. Current politics and macro: identify the exact policy/geopolitical event, why it matters for liquidity/risk appetite/demand/cost, and whether it is confirmed or rumor.
2. Policy transmission: connect policy to investable sectors through concrete mechanisms such as capex, subsidies, procurement, grid connection, export controls, tariff relief, or demand pull-forward.
3. Industry cycle: state where the sector sits in the cycle: early recovery, acceleration, overheating, peaking, inventory digestion, or downcycle.
4. Financial proof: check representative companies' latest revenue growth, profit growth, gross margin, cash flow, inventory, capex, orders/backlog, and guidance when available.
5. Valuation and crowding: compare recent price move with earnings revisions, valuation percentiles if available, fund positioning, ETF premium/volume, and media heat.
6. Capital and technical confirmation: use main-fund flow, ETF flow, breadth, K-line, volume/price, moving averages, support/resistance, and relative strength.
7. Actionability: map the thesis to funds/ETFs/active funds, then state position role, entry trigger, invalidation, and why not to buy.

## Event Impact Map

For daily market, mainline, and opportunity-mining answers, create a compact event map before the sector call:

- Event: name the geopolitical, policy, summit, central-bank, commodity, earnings, or regulatory catalyst.
- Status: confirmed, scheduled, rumor, denied, or already completed.
- Market channel: liquidity, risk appetite, export/import restrictions, fiscal spending, subsidies, procurement, demand, costs, exchange rate, commodity price, or valuation multiple.
- Bullish sectors and bearish sectors: specify first-order and second-order beneficiaries/losers.
- Priced-in check: compare the event with recent K-line extension, media heat, capital flow, and ETF volume.
- Contradiction: state whether current tape confirms the event narrative or rejects it.

Do not summarize an event as simply "利好科技" or "利空消费". Always explain the transmission path.

## Financial Report Checks

When a sector is being considered for加仓 or主线:

- Use recent quarterly/annual reports and official announcements when possible.
- Prefer sector leaders and fund top holdings as proxies, but say when using proxies.
- Look for direction, not false precision: improving orders/margins/cash flow is stronger than one-off revenue growth.
- For cyclical sectors such as lithium, coal, shipping, solar, and semiconductors, include product price, inventory, capacity utilization, and capex cycle.
- For high-growth technology such as CPO, AI hardware, robotics, semiconductors, and commercial space, include order visibility, customer concentration, export restrictions, capex, gross margin, and whether earnings can catch up with valuation.
- For utilities/power/grid, include power demand, power prices, utilization hours, grid capex, regulated returns, fuel costs, and debt/cash-flow pressure.

## Opportunity Mining

Look beyond the obvious hot label:

- Find second-order beneficiaries: for AI, check power/grid/liquid cooling/PCB/optical components; for robots, check reducers/actuators/sensors/software; for commercial space, check satellite manufacturing/ground equipment/components/materials.
- For geopolitical events, look for supply-chain substitution, sanctions/export-control beneficiaries, defense/security demand, shipping/energy/commodity shocks, and risk-hedge assets; then verify with K-line and capital flow.
- For major summits or policy meetings, distinguish "pre-event expectation trade", "during-event confirmation", and "post-event sell-the-news". Downgrade sectors that already had parabolic K-lines before the event unless new policy details are stronger than expected.
- Separate "front-row" from "back-row": front-row themes have strongest资金 and consensus; back-row opportunities need improving fundamentals plus early资金 confirmation.
- Search for divergence: sector up but ETF/fund not crowded, policy strong but price not extended, financials improving while market narrative still cold.
- Prefer "quietly improving + data confirmation" over "already viral + one-day spike" for埋伏.

## Anti-Formula Output Rules

- Do not answer every question with the same table. Use tables only where comparison improves clarity.
- Give one decisive view, then explain the evidence chain and the key反证.
- Include a "我可能错在哪里" sentence for high-uncertainty calls.
- For next-day predictions, provide scenario probabilities plus exact trigger levels or conditions, not just涨/跌.
- For daily market reviews, include both event/policy interpretation and technical interpretation of major indexes/hot sectors; if one is missing, state that the view is incomplete.
- For portfolio advice, tailor the action to the user's actual持仓, cash, and recent decisions.
- Highlight one non-obvious detail that is not already visible from涨跌幅 alone.

## Confidence Labels

- High confidence: policy, financials,资金, and技术 all align.
- Medium confidence: two or three pillars align, with one unresolved contradiction.
- Low confidence: mainly event-driven or tape-driven, with weak财报/估值 support.

Always reduce position-size advice when confidence is below high.
